## How to Use This Roadmap

- **Follow the Timeline:** Start with fundamentals, then progressively specialize and gain clients.
- **Practice Daily:** Dedicate consistent time to learning, creating, and engaging.
- **Build Your Portfolio:** Showcase diverse, high-quality projects with process case studies.
- **Start Freelancing:** Use platforms, outreach, and social media to land your first clients.
- **Scale & Grow:** Increase your rates, expand your services, and consider building an agency.
