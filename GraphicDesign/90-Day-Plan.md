## 🎯 Your 90-Day Action Plan

### **Month 1: Foundation**
```
Week 1: Learn design fundamentals (elements + principles)
Week 2: Master 1 software (Canva or Figma - free)
Week 3: Study 100 designs, recreate 10
Week 4: Create 5 portfolio pieces

Goal: Understand design + basic software skills
```

### **Month 2: Skill Building**
```
Week 5: Learn advanced software (Photoshop or Illustrator)
Week 6: Choose specialization (logo, social media, UI/UX)
Week 7: Create 10 pieces in your specialization
Week 8: Build portfolio website

Goal: 15 portfolio pieces + professional presence
```

### **Month 3: Getting Clients**
```
Week 9: Set up Fiverr + Upwork profiles
Week 10: Do 2 free projects for testimonials
Week 11: Direct outreach (50 businesses)
Week 12: Close first 3 paying clients

Goal: $500-1000 first month income
```
