## 🎯 Design Fields & Specializations {#fields}

### **1. Logo Design & Branding**

**What It Is:**
Creating visual identity for businesses/products

**Skills Needed:**
- Vector graphics (Illustrator)
- Typography
- Color theory
- Understanding brand strategy

**Deliverables:**
- Logo (primary, secondary, icon)
- Color palette
- Typography system
- Brand guidelines
- Business cards, letterheads

**Earning Potential:**
- Beginner: $50-200 per logo
- Intermediate: $200-1000
- Expert: $1000-10,000+

**Why Choose This:**
- ✅ High demand
- ✅ Can charge premium
- ✅ Build long-term client relationships
- ✅ Portfolio-building friendly

**How to Start:**
1. Study 100 famous logos (analyze why they work)
2. Create 50 practice logos (different industries)
3. Learn logo design process (research → sketching → refinement)
4. Build logo portfolio (10-15 pieces)

**Best Resources:**
- [Logo Design Course](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zOpaRSWeMaI) - Satori Graphics
- [Logo Design Love](https://www.logodesignlove.com/) - Blog
- [Logobook](https://www.logobook.com/) - Inspiration

**Practice Projects:**
- Coffee shop logo
- Tech startup logo
- Restaurant logo
- Fitness brand logo
- Personal branding

### **2. Social Media Design**

**What It Is:**
Creating graphics for social platforms

**Platforms:**
- Instagram (posts, stories, reels)
- Facebook (covers, ads)
- Twitter/X (headers, posts)
- LinkedIn (banners, carousels)
- Pinterest (pins)
- TikTok (thumbnails)

**Standard Sizes:**
```
Instagram Post: 1080x1080px
Instagram Story: 1080x1920px
Facebook Cover: 820x312px
Twitter Header: 1500x500px
LinkedIn Banner: 1584x396px
Pinterest Pin: 1000x1500px
YouTube Thumbnail: 1280x720px
```

**Skills Needed:**
- Photoshop or Canva
- Understanding of each platform's style
- Typography
- On-brand design

**Earning Potential:**
- Per post: $10-50
- Monthly package (30 posts): $300-1500
- Full management: $1000-5000/month

**Why Choose This:**
- ✅ Consistent recurring work
- ✅ Low barrier to entry
- ✅ High demand (every business needs this)
- ✅ Quick turnaround projects

**How to Start:**
1. Create templates in Canva/Photoshop
2. Study successful accounts in different niches
3. Offer free work to 2-3 businesses for portfolio
4. Create package deals (10, 20, 30 posts)

**Best Resources:**
- [Social Media Design Course](https://www.skillshare.com/browse/social-media-design) - Skillshare
- [Canva Design School](https://www.canva.com/learn/design/) - Canva

**Practice Projects:**
- 30-day content calendar for cafe
- Instagram carousel for course
- LinkedIn carousel for tips
- Story template set

### **3. UI/UX Design**

**What It Is:**
Designing user interfaces and experiences for digital products

**UI (User Interface):**
- Visual design of screens
- Buttons, icons, colors
- Typography, layout

**UX (User Experience):**
- User research
- User flows and wireframes
- Usability testing
- Information architecture

**Skills Needed:**
- Figma (primary tool)
- Prototyping
- User research
- Basic HTML/CSS understanding

**Deliverables:**
- Wireframes
- High-fidelity mockups
- Interactive prototypes
- Design system/style guide

**Earning Potential:**
- Landing page: $300-2000
- Mobile app: $2000-10,000+
- Full website: $1000-20,000+
- Hourly: $30-150/hour

**Why Choose This:**
- ✅ Highest paying design field
- ✅ Growing demand (tech boom)
- ✅ Remote-friendly
- ✅ Can transition to product designer

**How to Start:**
1. Learn Figma basics (1 week)
2. Study existing apps (analyze UI patterns)
3. Redesign existing websites (practice)
4. Learn UX fundamentals (user flows, research)
5. Build 3-5 case studies for portfolio

**Best Resources:**
- [Google UX Design Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-ux-design) - Coursera (7 months)
- [UI/UX Design Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c9Wg6Cb_YlU) - DesignCourse
- [Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/) - Principles
- [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/) - UI inspiration
- [Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/) - Inspiration

**Practice Projects:**
- Redesign a popular app
- Design a to-do app
- Create e-commerce checkout flow
- Design food delivery app
- Build personal portfolio website

### **4. Print Design**

**What It Is:**
Designing for physical printing

**Types:**
- Business cards
- Brochures and flyers
- Posters
- Packaging
- Book covers
- Magazines

**Skills Needed:**
- InDesign (primary)
- Illustrator (graphics)
- Photoshop (images)
- Understanding print specs (CMYK, bleed, resolution)

**Print Terms to Know:**
```
DPI: Dots per inch (300 for print, 72 for web)
CMYK: Print color mode (vs RGB for screens)
Bleed: Extra 3mm around edge for cutting
Trim: Final size after cutting
Safe Zone: Area safe from cutting (5mm inside)
```

**Earning Potential:**
- Business card: $50-200
- Brochure: $200-800
- Poster: $100-500
- Book cover: $300-2000
- Packaging: $500-5000+

**Why Choose This:**
- ✅ Tangible results
- ✅ Local business demand
- ✅ Can specialize (packaging, editorial)
- ✅ Premium pricing for print

**How to Start:**
1. Learn InDesign basics
2. Understand print specifications
3. Create templates for common items
4. Partner with local print shop
5. Offer combo packages (business card + flyer + brochure)

**Best Resources:**
- [InDesign Basics](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLYfCBK8IplO6v0QjCj-TSrFUXnRV0WxfE) - Envato
- [Print Design Specs](https://www.printivity.com/insights/print-design-specs/) - Printivity

**Practice Projects:**
- Business card set
- Event poster
- Restaurant menu
- Magazine spread
- Product packaging mockup

### **6. Motion Graphics**

**What It Is:**
Animated graphics and visual effects

**Types:**
- Logo animations
- Social media animations
- Explainer videos
- Title sequences
- GIFs

**Skills Needed:**
- After Effects (primary)
- Illustrator (assets)
- Understanding of animation principles

**Earning Potential:**
- Logo animation: $100-500
- Social media animation: $50-300
- Explainer video: $500-3000
- Per second of animation: $50-200

**Why Choose This:**
- ✅ High demand (video content boom)
- ✅ Premium pricing
- ✅ Less competition than static design
- ✅ Impressive portfolio pieces

**How to Start:**
1. Learn After Effects basics (2 weeks)
2. Understand 12 principles of animation
3. Start with simple logo animations
4. Progress to complex motion graphics
5. Create demo reel

**Best Resources:**
- [After Effects for Beginners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vQ-BCep8PNI) - Ben Marriott
- [School of Motion](https://www.schoolofmotion.com/) - Courses
- [Motion Design School](https://motiondesign.school/) - Tutorials

### **Recommended Specialization Path:**

**Fast Money:**
```
Start: Social Media Design (quick clients)
Add: Logo Design (higher prices)
Expand: Full Branding (premium service)
```

**High Income:**
```
Focus: UI/UX Design
Timeline: 3-6 months to freelance
Potential: $3000-10,000/month
```

**Creative Freedom:**
```
Focus: Illustration
Build: Unique style
Monetize: Commissions + products
```

**Balanced Approach:**
```
Month 1-2: Social Media + Logo Design
Month 3-4: Add Print Design
Month 5-6: Learn UI/UX
Result: Diverse income streams
```
