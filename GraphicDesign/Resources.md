### **Books (Essential Reading)**

1. **"The Non-Designer's Design Book"** - Robin Williams
   - Perfect for beginners
   - CRAP principles (Contrast, Repetition, Alignment, Proximity)

2. **"Logo Design Love"** - David Airey
   - Logo design process
   - Case studies
   - [Free Online](https://www.logodesignlove.com/)

3. **"Don't Make Me Think"** - Steve Krug
   - UX/Usability fundamentals
   - Essential for UI/UX designers

4. **"Thinking with Type"** - Ellen Lupton
   - Typography bible
   - Essential for all designers

5. **"The Elements of Typographic Style"** - Robert Bringhurst
   - Advanced typography
